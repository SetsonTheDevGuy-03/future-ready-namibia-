BEHIND THE PROJECT

Built for opportunity.
Created for learning.

FutureReady Namibia is a student education project created to share realistic career information and useful learning pathways, especially for rural school leavers.

OUR PURPOSE

Useful information, without false promises.

Youth unemployment is complex. Our goal is to present information in clear language and give readers approachable ways to practise career skills. Online opportunities may help some learners, but reliable infrastructure, fair hiring and quality education are also essential.

We focus on practical, accessible learning rather than claims of quick or guaranteed earnings.

EDITORIAL APPROACH

What you should know.

Research and sourcing. Our content uses general labour-market explanations, clearly identified hypothetical examples and links to established institutions and free-learning providers. We deliberately avoid stating unverified current Namibian unemployment rates.

Independence. FutureReady Namibia is an educational student project. External links are informational and do not imply sponsorship, employment placement or endorsement by the organisations listed.

Accessibility. The site aims to work on phones, uses readable text and includes downloadable audio for listeners with limited connectivity. The video features on-screen educational text and a written summary.

Respect and privacy. This site has no backend and does not collect form submissions. The CV builder processes entries in-browser only; the optional planner stores its checked steps in local browser storage.

Lecturer requirement: The ITL60US/IDT60US assignment says groups of five. The five members are listed above. Please confirm names, student numbers, programmes and group contributions before submission.
Photography note: Images used throughout this educational website are illustrative stock photographs from Unsplash. They do not depict the group members or verified participants in Namibian employment programmes.
