The message behind the video

Every young person deserves the opportunity to learn and contribute. Digital skills can help create new pathways, but opportunity also depends on access to education, devices, connectivity and fair employment systems.

Choose one practical skill. Learn using trusted resources. Build a small project that demonstrates your progress. Keep exploring legitimate work and training options—and remain cautious about promises of instant online money.

This is not a guarantee of employment. It is an invitation to start with one useful step.
