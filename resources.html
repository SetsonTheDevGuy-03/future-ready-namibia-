STAY SAFE ONLINE

Could you spot a job scam?

Try five realistic scenarios. Remember: legitimate work does not require sharing account passwords, and guaranteed easy earnings are a warning sign.

Read our safety article

1. Someone promises a large payment for 10 minutes of work but asks for a registration deposit.

2. You find an online job but cannot tell if Namibian residents can register or withdraw money.

3. A supposed recruiter asks for your banking password over a chat app.

4. A company logo appears on a message containing a job offer.

5. A project asks you to post misleading reviews of a product.
